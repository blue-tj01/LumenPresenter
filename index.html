        Open on another device

          Each link carries the access token, so the device opening it needs nothing else.

          Access token

          A device opening Lumen without a link is asked for this token. New makes another one and signs every other device out.

          Signed-in devices

            Disconnect signs a device out. Anyone who still knows the token can sign back in, so make a New token to keep them out.

            How to write slides

            [Verse 1] on its own line starts a group.

            A blank line starts a new slide inside the group.

            Groups named Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Pre-Chorus, Tag, Intro or Ending get colour-coded automatically.

            Optional first line @bg: #hex or an image URL sets the presentation's default background.

            @image: … on a slide shows a picture on it, with any other lines on that slide as text over it.

            @video: … on a slide plays a video on it, once, with sound on the audience output.

            Add images / videos (or drop files onto the text) adds one slide each, which makes a slideshow.

            Play order lists groups in the order they're sung, e.g. Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2, Chorus. Click a group below to add it. Leave it empty to play the groups as written.

            Written by, Copyright and CCLI song # are shown on the song's last slide, with your church's CCLI licence number from the Theme tab. Search finds songs by any of them, and by their words.

            Auto-advance moves to the next slide by itself after that many seconds, and after the last slide in the play order goes back to the first. Video slides move on when the video ends. Leave it empty to advance by hand.
